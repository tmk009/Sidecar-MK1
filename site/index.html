Setup & status What it does

A Raspberry Pi connects to the MK1 over USB and runs the sound engines. A modified Rytm OS brings them into the machine list. Their audio returns through the Rytm’s own track processing, including its analog filter and amp.

Select a machine, turn the encoders and play it from the pads or sequencer. The interface is adapted to the MK1’s 122 × 32 monochrome display.

VOICES

Digital machines on your tracks

Choose a Sidecar machine from a track’s native machine list. The current MK1 scope includes seven engines, from percussion and bass to wavetable textures and resonant samples.

PLAITS
Multi-model digital synthesis.
MD
Digital percussion from mdkit.
ACID
Acid-style bass synthesis.
WAVEPAD
Wavetable colors and morphing textures.
JUNO
Synth voices with 128 patch foundations.
PLUCK
Plucked-string synthesis.
SAMPLE
Rytm sample playback with a resonator.

Available across twelve logical tracks, sharing eight physical voices and the native choke pairs.

ON THE MK1

The Rytm is the interface

The SYNTH page puts the machine’s parameters on the eight encoders. Machines with additional controls use the SAMPLE page too. Play from the pads, program a pattern and shape the sound with the track’s filter and amp.

The pages are designed for the original MK1 display. You can select and play the machines directly on the Rytm while the Pi handles synthesis.

SAMPLESYNTH & SAMPLE

SAMPLE

Your samples, through a resonator

SAMPLE uses the Rytm’s native sample source and displays its waveform. Adjust tuning, playback direction, start, end and loop position, then blend the sample with the resonator using MIX.

Its sample and resonator controls occupy the native pages, keeping playback and sound shaping on the instrument.

Some factory sample sources are currently inaccessible. See supported scope and limitations.

SEQUENCING & MODULATION

Parameter locks and the native LFO

Use the Rytm’s sequencer to trigger digital voices and lock supported machine parameters per step. The native LFO connection sends changing parameter values to the Pi without retriggering the voice.

The implementation covers the active machines and all sixteen SAMPLE controls, including sample direction. Sample selection stays in the native browser.

In testing LFO and parameter-lock testing on hardware is ongoing. SAMPLE DIR does not yet support parameter locks.

AUDIO

Digital voices, analog track processing

The Pi returns the generated audio over USB. On the Rytm, it passes through the track’s overdrive, filter, AMP and pan before reaching the mix.
